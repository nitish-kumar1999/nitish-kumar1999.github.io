## Research Interests

My research interests lie broadly in the areas of **earthquake engineering, structural dynamics, seismic wave propagation, and vibration monitoring**. My work combines experimental investigations, signal processing, analytical methods, and numerical approaches to study the dynamic behaviour of structures and ground motion.

### Earthquake Engineering

- Ground motion characterization and analysis
- Seismic response of structures
- Earthquake-induced vibrations
- Seismic hazard and ground-motion studies

### Structural Dynamics

- Dynamic response of structures
- SDOF and MDOF systems
- Structural vibration and response analysis
- Soil–structure interaction

### Seismic Wave Propagation

- Seismic wave propagation
- Body and surface waves
- Wave propagation through soil and structural systems
- Rotational ground motion

### Vibration Monitoring and Signal Processing

- Experimental vibration measurements
- Seismic instrumentation
- Time- and frequency-domain analysis
- Signal processing of seismic and vibration data

### Offshore and Coastal Engineering

- Offshore structures
- Coastal engineering
- Offshore renewable energy
- Dynamic response of offshore systems
