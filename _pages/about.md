## Welcome to my academic website

I am **Nitish Kumar**, a PhD Scholar in the **Department of Earthquake Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee (IIT Roorkee), India**.

My research interests broadly focus on **earthquake engineering, structural dynamics, seismic wave propagation, vibration monitoring, and offshore/coastal engineering**. My current research involves understanding structural and ground-motion response using experimental measurements, signal processing, and numerical approaches.

### Research Interests

- Earthquake Engineering
- Structural Dynamics
- Seismic Wave Propagation
- Ground Motion and Seismic Response
- Vibration Monitoring and Signal Processing
- Soil–Structure Interaction
- Offshore Structures
- Coastal Engineering
- Offshore Renewable Energy

### Current Research

My research work involves experimental and analytical investigation of **ground and structural vibrations**, with particular interest in wave propagation, rotational ground motion, and the dynamic response of structures.

I am also interested in the application of **experimental measurements, numerical modelling, and signal-processing techniques** to understand vibration characteristics and structural response.
