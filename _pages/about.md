## Welcome to my academic website

I am **Nitish Kumar**, a PhD Scholar in the **Department of Earthquake Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee (IIT Roorkee), India**.

My academic and research journey is centered on understanding problems related to earthquake engineering and the dynamic behaviour of structures and infrastructure. My work involves experimental investigations, analytical methods, numerical modelling, and data analysis.

I am currently pursuing my doctoral research at IIT Roorkee and am involved in research activities related to the dynamic behaviour of structures and ground motion.

*This website is used to share my research, publications, academic activities, projects, and professional information.*
